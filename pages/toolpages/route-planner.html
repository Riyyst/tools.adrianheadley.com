    About this tool

    What it does

    Plan and optimise a driving route containing multiple stops using postcodes, full addresses or coordinates.

    How to use it

    1. Enter the starting point, stops and optional final destination.
    2. Choose whether to return to the start where applicable, then plan the route.
    3. Review the optimised itinerary and map, copy it, open it in Google Maps or share the setup.

    Privacy and processing

    Routing requires online geocoding, mapping and routing services. Entered locations are sent to those services to calculate and display the route.
