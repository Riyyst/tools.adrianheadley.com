About this tool

What it does

Convert a value between compatible measurement units.

How to use it

  1. Enter the value you want to convert.
  2. Choose the source and destination units and any supported compound-unit options.
  3. Read or copy the converted result, or share the configured conversion.

Privacy and processing

Unit conversion is calculated locally in your browser and entered values are not intentionally uploaded by the tool.
