About this tool

What it does

Search through text and replace matching words, phrases or characters quickly.

How to use it

  1. Paste, type or import the text you want to edit.
  2. Enter the text to find and the replacement, then choose any matching options.
  3. Replace one or all matches, then copy or download the updated text.

Privacy and processing

Text editing is performed in your browser. Imported supported documents are processed locally by the page.
