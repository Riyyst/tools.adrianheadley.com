About this tool

What it does

Convert a date and time from one time zone into another.

How to use it

  1. Choose the source date, time and time zone.
  2. Choose the destination time zone.
  3. Review the converted time or copy a setup link.

Privacy and processing

Time-zone conversion runs in your browser. The dates and times you enter are not intentionally uploaded by the tool.
