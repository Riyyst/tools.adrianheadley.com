About this tool

What it does

Compare two pieces of text and highlight what has been added, removed or changed.

How to use it

  1. Paste or load the original text into the first side.
  2. Paste or load the revised text into the second side.
  3. Review the highlighted additions, removals and other differences.

Privacy and processing

Text comparison is performed in your browser and the compared text is not intentionally uploaded by the tool.
