Privacy

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no stored tool data.

Our Commitment

Welcome to tools.adrianheadley.com. Privacy is built into the way the site works. Most tools process information locally inside your browser. Tools that require live internet data may contact an independent service directly from your browser.

Browser-Only Processing

Local tools process text, images, documents and other information on your device. Live-data tools may send the value needed for the lookup directly from your browser to the relevant independent service.

  • No tools.adrianheadley.com processing server — The website does not operate a separate backend that receives or stores your tool inputs.
  • Website hosting only — The hosting server delivers the HTML, CSS, JavaScript and other files needed to open the website.
  • Local files stay local — Files opened in browser-based tools remain on your device unless a tool clearly uses or links to an independent external service.
  • Live lookups — Some tools contact public or third-party services directly from your browser. The searched domain, URL, address or other value needed for that lookup may therefore be sent to that service.
  • No retained tool data by this site — tools.adrianheadley.com does not intentionally save or retain the information entered into the tools.

Cookies, Analytics and Tracking

  • No cookies — The website does not use cookies for analytics, advertising, personalisation or tracking.
  • No analytics — No first-party analytics or advertising tracker is intentionally loaded by Adrian Headley Tools, and no analytics service is used to monitor visitors or measure individual behaviour.
  • No tracking — No tracking pixels, fingerprinting scripts or cross-site identifiers are included.
  • No advertising profiles — User activity is not used to create profiles or target advertising.
  • No sale or sharing of data — Tool data is not collected, so it cannot be sold, rented or shared.

Open-Source and Inspectable Code

The client-side code is openly accessible and can be inspected. This allows users to review how each tool works and confirm that processing takes place locally in the browser.

Local Browser Storage

Some tools may use temporary browser memory or local browser storage when needed for a feature, such as retaining an in-progress setting or locally created wallet. This information remains under the control of your browser and is not transmitted to tools.adrianheadley.com. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Live Data Services

Some tools need live public information. Username, Name & Contact OSINT may use AllOrigins, Jina Reader, public search-result pages and the Internet Archive Wayback Machine to check current and archived public profile pages and cross-reference user-supplied public details. Email and phone searches are limited to publicly indexed references and do not use account-recovery or contact-syncing methods. Other live-data tools use their own public or third-party services. These services are independent of tools.adrianheadley.com and may apply their own privacy, logging, rate-limit and retention practices.

IP & ASN Lookup may query RIPEstat, Google DNS-over-HTTPS and ipwho.is for public network, reverse-DNS and approximate IP geolocation information. The IP address or ASN entered may therefore be sent to those independent services.

Email Domain Analyzer may query Google DNS-over-HTTPS, AllOrigins for a public MTA-STS policy fallback, and Mail.gw for its current public temporary-email-domain list. The domain entered may therefore be sent to those independent services. It does not probe specific mailboxes or test SMTP recipients.

Public Document Finder may send the generated public search query to DuckDuckGo or Bing result pages through browser/public-content fallbacks, and may query the Internet Archive CDX service when archive searching is selected. Search terms and any domain filter may therefore be sent to those independent services. The tool searches public/indexed material and does not brute-force hidden directories.

Tool-Specific Browser Processing

Browser Code Studio keeps project files, imported code, project-wide search and replace data, local coaching analysis, queued console input and saved sessions in the browser unless you explicitly export them. The enhanced Monaco editor and Pyodide Python runtime are loaded from public jsDelivr CDN resources when available, and Python may download compatible packages needed by imports. If you choose Prepare offline mode, editor and runtime assets are stored in browser Cache Storage for later offline use. Those independent CDN requests are subject to the provider's own logging and privacy practices. The tool does not upload project source to tools.adrianheadley.com.

PDF Editor processes uploaded PDF files, annotations, signatures and stamps locally in the browser. PDF.js, pdf-lib and @pdf-lib/fontkit are loaded from public jsDelivr CDN resources when the editor is opened, but the PDF contents are not uploaded to tools.adrianheadley.com or to those libraries by the tool. Requests for the library files are subject to jsDelivr's own logging and privacy practices.

External Websites

The Powerful Websites directory and some tool pages may contain links to independent third-party websites. Those websites are separate from tools.adrianheadley.com and may use their own servers, cookies, analytics or data practices. Review their privacy policies before using them.

Accounts and Personal Information

No account is required to use the hosted tools. tools.adrianheadley.com does not ask for names, email addresses, payment details or other personal information to use the tool collection.

Changes to This Page

This page may be updated when the website changes. Any future feature that changes how information is handled will be explained clearly before it is introduced.
